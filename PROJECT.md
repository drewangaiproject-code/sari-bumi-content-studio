# Project Foundation — SARI BUMI Content Studio

## Purpose

Build a repeatable, reviewable workflow for SARI BUMI social content, with strong short-form writing and SVG/motion design as a visual differentiator.

The system should make jamu feel relevant to contemporary everyday life without making unsupported product, sensory, or health claims.

## Brand direction

- Working direction: **Modern Jamu / Perception Reset**.
- Explain and present the product as a contemporary beverage brand, not as medicine.
- Prefer concrete, culturally familiar details and a clear creative idea over generic marketing language.
- Use motion and visual storytelling when they strengthen the idea, not merely as decoration.

## Content pillars

These are the current strategic framework and should not be renamed without Owner approval:

1. **P1 — The Wah Factor** (~30%): surprising, scroll-stopping creative ideas.
2. **P2 — Quality You Can See** (~25%): show credible, observable product or process details only when evidence/assets support them.
3. **P3 — Real Jamu, Made Legible** (~25%): make ingredients, context, and product information easier to understand without implying unverified benefits.
4. **P4 — Everyday Ritual & Participation** (~20%): everyday moments and audience participation.

Percentages are planning hypotheses, not performance guarantees.

## Audience and format hypotheses

- Audience hypothesis: younger browsers, with a possible women-skew; this is not a verified audience fact.
- Starting format hypothesis: Reels 45%, Carousels 35%, Images 20%.
- Starting content mix hypothesis: 75% core concepts, 25% experiments.
- Review these hypotheses against actual account data when a usable baseline exists.

## Creative and factual guardrails

- Use natural Indonesian; avoid generic, overblown, or defensive marketing copy.
- A hook must create a specific question, contrast, or curiosity that the content actually resolves.
- Do not invent ingredients, manufacturing details, taste, freshness, quality, customer reactions, certifications, or product effects.
- Do not make medical, disease-treatment, prevention, dosage, or other health claims without explicit substantiation and approval.
- Do not imply an ingredient list is exhaustive unless a verified product source says so.
- Never mix the ingredients or details of different products.
- Do not show ingredient counts in public visuals unless the Owner explicitly revises this rule; the previous project marked this as `OMIT_FROM_PUBLIC_VISUAL`.
- Keep product facts, creative hypotheses, and unverified ideas clearly separated.
