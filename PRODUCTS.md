## Evidence register

For every product fact or proposed claim used in content, record:
- Exact fact or claim.
- Product/SKU it applies to.
- Source file, label, approved record, or other evidence.
- Date checked and reviewer.
- Status: `CONFIRMED`, `PENDING`, or `REJECTED`.
- Scope/wording allowed for public use, if confirmed.
- Expiry or recheck condition, if relevant.

## Claim rules

- No medical or therapeutic promises, disease treatment/prevention claims, dosage guidance, or implied clinical effects without appropriate evidence and explicit approval.
- Do not infer health benefits from an ingredient's traditional use.
- Do not invent taste, texture, freshness, quality, production method, sourcing, certification, or customer experience.
- A factual ingredient statement must match the verified record and must not imply completeness if the source does not support that implication.
- If evidence is missing or ambiguous, omit the claim or ask the Owner. Do not fill gaps creatively.
- Product information in an old draft is not evidence by itself.
